Multiplayer co-op for Timberborn: build one colony together in real time, with Steam friend invites, an in-game connection panel and a long list of crash and desync fixes.

[b]Guides:[/b] [url=https://timbermods.github.io/BeaverBuddies-Stability-Fork/install.html]install[/url] · [url=https://timbermods.github.io/BeaverBuddies-Stability-Fork/troubleshooting.html]troubleshooting[/url] · [url=https://timbermods.github.io/BeaverBuddies-Stability-Fork/]website[/url]

[b]What you get[/b]
[list]
[*] One shared colony, each player with their own camera. Multi-start maps give each player a start.
[*] Steam invites from the overlay, with no port forwarding. Direct IP works too.
[*] A connection panel with each player's ping, sync status and chat.
[*] Teammates' cursors, and map pings.
[*] Fixes for water, animation, random numbers, saving, demolition, Wonders, input and the network.
[/list]

[b]Before you play[/b]
[list]
[*] Requires Harmony and Mod Settings, and Timberborn 1.1.2.4.
[*] Every player needs the same version of this mod.
[*] Enable only one BeaverBuddies: this fork, the original, or Timber Together.
[*] Play on a copy of your save. A desync can still happen.
[/list]

[b]Found a problem?[/b] Send every player's Player.log to [url=https://github.com/timbermods/BeaverBuddies-Stability-Fork/issues]this fork's issues[/url], not to the original project. The [url=https://timbermods.github.io/BeaverBuddies-Stability-Fork/troubleshooting.html]troubleshooting guide[/url] says where the log is.
